Collection: Asset
Purpose: Vehicles/assets: identity, specs, documents/expiry, device link.
Use for: Plate lookup, active/inactive fleet list, vehicle specs, document expiry flags, device link.
Do not confuse with: Not live GPS (that is AvlRecordLatest). Not trips (Trip). Not the physical tracker record (Device).
Tenant field: yes (sub_tenant_id)
IDs: _id (asset id); details.plate_number; device_details.imei (->Device.imei)
Default sections: asset_base, asset_details_base
Enums:
- status: Active | Blocked | Inactive | Maintenance | Migrated
- device_availability: AssignedToDevice | AvailableForDevice
- availability: Assigned | Available
- installation_type: Basic | PTA | SpecializedPTA
- details.plate_type: Private | PrivateCar | PrivateTransport | PublicTransport | HeavyEquipment (seen)
- details.vehicle_type: Car | PickUp | 3Ton PickUp | Van | Truck | Trailer | Tanker | SchoolBus | Generator | Fleet (seen)
- details.custom_optional.register_on_wasl: true | false
Notes:
- Plate number lives at details.plate_number (not top-level).
- Fleeti "active vehicle" = AssignedToDevice + status Active/Maintenance + real device_details.imei + expiry_date in the future.
Sections:
- asset_base: name, status, availability, created_at, expiry_date, renewal_date
- asset_details_base: plate_number, plate_type, vehicle_type, fleet_type, make, model, VIN, fuel_type, odometer
- asset_details_cargo_load_management
- asset_details_cost_finance
- asset_details_custom_optional
- asset_details_document_status_flags: insurance/istimara/mvpi/operation-card/barrier-cert expiry & status
- asset_details_maintenance_logs
- asset_details_ownership_operations
- asset_details_safety_security
- asset_details_special_equipment
- asset_details_vehicle_capacity_specialization
- asset_device_details: device_details.imei (->Device.imei), group_id (->AssetGroup._id), group_name, device_name
- asset_extra: other fields (audit: created/updated by/at, and less common fields)
Indexes:
- device_availability + status + device_details.imei + sub_tenant_id
- expiry_date
- renewal_date
- installation_date

- sub_tenant_id + renewal_date
- sub_tenant_id + installation_date
