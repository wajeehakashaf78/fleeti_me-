Collection: AvlRecordLatestFields
Purpose: Current IO/sensor values per IMEI, flattened (BLE temp/humidity/battery, voltage, fuel %, door).
Use for: Current sensor/IO values without digging nested AvlRecordLatest structure.
Do not confuse with: Not a full trip history; companion to AvlRecordLatest keyed by imei.
Tenant field: yes (sub_tenant_id)
IDs: imei (unique); device_context.asset_id (->Asset._id)
Default sections: AvlRecordLatestFields_base
Notes:
- Values are strings - convert with $toDouble before comparing numbers.
- Unique index on imei. Companion snapshot of latest IO/sensor fields.
Sections:
- AvlRecordLatestFields_base: timestamp, speed, ignition, battery, voltage, fuel %, door, temperature
- AvlRecordLatestFields_ble: BLE sensor slots 1-10: temperature, humidity, battery
- AvlRecordLatestFields_raw: raw / processing fields of the packet (debugging; large values)
- AvlRecordLatestFields_extra: other fields (audit: created/updated by/at, and less common fields)
Indexes:
- imei (unique)
