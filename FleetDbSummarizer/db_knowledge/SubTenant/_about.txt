Collection: SubTenant
Purpose: Customer / account master inside the fms database. One SubTenant = one company account that owns vehicles, drivers, devices, SIMs, etc.
Use for: Who the customer is, account status, max devices, contact, VAT / CR number, account manager, billing, branding.
Do not confuse with: Not the vehicles themselves - those are in Asset. Other collections point here via sub_tenant_id.
Tenant field: is the tenant itself: SubTenant.sub_account_id = sub_tenant_id of every other collection
IDs: sub_account_id (= sub_tenant_id everywhere else; hidden, applied by the server)
Default sections: SubTenant_base
Hidden fields: password, username, sub_account_id
Enums:
- status: Active
- subtenant_status: Active | Suspended
Notes:
- Other collections reference the tenant with sub_tenant_id (UUID-like id or host string such as fms.tracking.me).
- SubTenant documents themselves do not carry sub_tenant_id; they ARE the tenant.
- LIMITED: credentials (password, username) and the internal account id are hidden.
- The current user's own tenant only - never list other tenants.
- The server returns only the current user's own account row.
Sections:
- SubTenant_base
- SubTenant_extra: other fields (audit: created/updated by/at, and less common fields)
Indexes:
- container_id
- container_group_id
- sub_account_id
- container_group_id + sub_account_id
- name
