Collection: Geofence
Purpose: Geofence zone DEFINITIONS (Circle/Polygon) and assigned assets/groups. Entry/exit logs live in AlertHistory.
Use for: Fence name, shape, radius, assigned vehicles.
Do not confuse with: Not entry/exit event logs (those live in AlertHistory, outside this focused set).
Tenant field: yes (sub_tenant_id)
IDs: _id; name; asset_assignments[].asset_id (->Asset._id); asset_group_assignments[].group_id (->AssetGroup._id)
Default sections: Geofence_base
Enums:
- type: Circle | Polygon
- status: Active | Inactive
Notes:
- Join: Geofence.asset_assignments[].asset_id = Asset._id.
- type Circle | Polygon. Circle uses radius (meters) + center in coordinates.
- This collection is DEFINITION only.
Sections:
- Geofence_base: name, type (Polygon/Circle), status, coordinates, radius
- Geofence_asset_assignment: assets & asset-groups assigned to the geofence (ids)
- Geofence_extra: other fields (audit: created/updated by/at, and less common fields)
