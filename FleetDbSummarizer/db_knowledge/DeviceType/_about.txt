Collection: DeviceType
Purpose: Device type / feature templates (name, features, protocols), often per sub-tenant.
Use for: Classify devices (Device.device_type_id / device_type).
Do not confuse with: Not the live tracker; not the same as DeviceModel.
Tenant field: no (sub_tenant_id is empty on every row) - reach it through Device
IDs: _id (->Device.device_type_id); name
Default sections: DeviceType_base
Notes:
- Join: Device.device_type_id = DeviceType._id.
- Often tenant-scoped; join Device.device_type_id = DeviceType._id.
Sections:
- DeviceType_base
- DeviceType_extra: other fields (audit: created/updated by/at, and less common fields)
