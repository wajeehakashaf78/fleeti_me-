Collection: AvlRecordLatest
Purpose: CURRENT telemetry: exactly one latest packet per DEVICE (live location, speed, ignition, movement).
Use for: Live map, online/offline, moving/idle/stopped right now.
Do not confuse with: Not history (AvlRecord). Do not sort the whole collection to find "latest" - every row already is the latest packet of its device.
Tenant field: yes (sub_tenant_id)
IDs: imei / device_id (both unique); device_context.asset_id (->Asset._id)
Default sections: AvlRecordLatest_base
Enums:
- ignition_status: On | Off
- movement: On | Off
- device_state.motion_state.status: Moving | Idling | Stopped | Illegal Movement | Unknown
Notes:
- One row per DEVICE (imei and device_id are unique). About 300 vehicles have 2+ rows (device swapped) and ~1,600 rows have no vehicle.
- Per vehicle: inside the $lookup from Asset add {"$sort":{"timestamp":-1}},{"$limit":1} after the match, so each vehicle counts once.
- Always match is_processed: true.
- Used for online (packet < 30 min), offline, not reporting, movement states.
Sections:
- AvlRecordLatest_base: timestamp, location, speed, ignition_status, movement, gps_status, odometer, motion_state
- AvlRecordLatest_sensor: current fuel/temperature/humidity/weight & other sensor readings
- AvlRecordLatest_raw: raw / processing fields of the packet (debugging; large values)
- AvlRecordLatest_extra: other fields (audit: created/updated by/at, and less common fields)
- AvlRecordLatest_movement: summary of the CURRENT movement - present on few rows; prefer Trip / DailyAnalytics for history
Indexes:
- imei + device_id + timestamp
- device_id + timestamp (unique)
- imei
- imei + is_processed
- imei + timestamp
- sub_tenant_id
