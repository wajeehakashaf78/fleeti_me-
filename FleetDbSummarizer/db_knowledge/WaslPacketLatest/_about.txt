Collection: WaslPacketLatest
Purpose: Latest WASL push pointer per IMEI (avl_record_id + timestamp) - companion to WaslPacket history.
Use for: Most recent WASL sync time / packet for a device.
Do not confuse with: Not full WASL push history (WaslPacket). Not current fleet map position (AvlRecordLatest).
Tenant field: no - scope through a join to a tenant-aware collection (Asset / Device / Driver)
IDs: imei (one row per device); avl_record_id (->AvlRecord._id)
Default sections: WaslPacketLatest_base
Notes:
- One latest pointer style doc per imei/tenant context.
- Join history with WaslPacket on imei (and optionally avl_record_id).
Sections:
- WaslPacketLatest_base
- WaslPacketLatest_extra: other fields (audit: created/updated by/at, and less common fields)
