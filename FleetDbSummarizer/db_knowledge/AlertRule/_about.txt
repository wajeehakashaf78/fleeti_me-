Collection: AlertRule
Purpose: User alert-rule DEFINITIONS/config (trigger logs live in AlertHistory). system_alert_rule=true marks system rules.
Use for: Which rules exist, their conditions, thresholds, schedules and which assets/groups they watch.
Do not confuse with: Not the triggered events (AlertHistory).
Tenant field: yes (sub_tenant_id)
IDs: _id (->AlertHistory.alert_info.alert_rule_id); basic_info.rule_name
Default sections: AlertRule_base
Enums:
- status: Active | Inactive
- basic_info.severity: Critical | Warning | Info
- condition.condition_category: ValueBased | EventBased | Logical | SystemOrHardware
- condition.event_based_conditions.geofence_type: Entry | Exit
- schedule.type: AlwaysActive | ActiveTimeWindow
- trigger.trigger_frequency: EveryTimeConditionIsMet | OnStateChangedNormalToAlert | OncePerDefinedPeriod
- notifications.notification_actions: Email | SMS | PushNotification | ShowWindowsPopup | Webhook
Sections:
- AlertRule_base: status, priority, system_alert_rule, basic_info.rule_name/description/severity/enable_rule
- AlertRule_assigned_assets: assigned_assets[].asset_id + assigned_asset_groups[].group_id monitored by the rule
- AlertRule_condition: value/event/logic/system based conditions & thresholds
- AlertRule_notifications
- AlertRule_schedule
- AlertRule_trigger
