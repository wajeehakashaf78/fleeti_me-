Collection: Driver
Purpose: Drivers: profile, vehicle assignments, license/visa/medical, compliance, analytics.
Use for: Driver name, assignment to a vehicle, overall safety/behavior KPIs, compliance docs.
Do not confuse with: Not per-trip stats (Trip). Not live location of the driver (comes via AvlRecord on the assigned vehicle).
Tenant field: yes (sub_tenant_id)
IDs: _id (driver id); profile.first_name + profile.last_name; asset_assignments[]._id (->Asset._id)
Default sections: Driver_base, Driver_Profile
Hidden fields: medical.blood_type, medical.allergies, medical.medications, medical.medicial_certification
Notes:
- Vehicle link: asset_assignments[]._id = Asset._id (use the latest assignment for current driver-of-vehicle).
- Overall KPIs are in driver_analytics (do not recompute from Trip for "overall" questions).
- Name = profile.first_name + profile.last_name.
Sections:
- Driver_base: core identity
- Driver_Profile: full personal/professional profile
- Driver_address_contact
- Driver_analytics: OVERALL/all-time driver KPIs (safety_score, behavior_score, avg_speed, total_trips/distance/hours, harsh-event counts) - use for 'overall'/all-time, do not recompute from Trip/DailyAnalytics
- Driver_asset_assignments: driver<->vehicle assignments (use latest for driver-of-vehicle)
- Driver_behavior: overall behavior scores (risk, compliance, fatigue)
- Driver_compliance
- Driver_emergency
- Driver_employment
- Driver_financial
- Driver_license: license issue/expiry/status
- Driver_medical: medical certificate expiry/status, fitness for duty (health details are private)
- Driver_performance: overall performance ratings (overall_rating, safety_score, efficiency)
- Driver_preferences
- Driver_status
- Driver_visa: visa/work-permit
- Driver_wasl_logs: WASL (Saudi) compliance logs
- Driver_extra: other fields (audit: created/updated by/at, and less common fields)
Indexes:
- sub_tenant_id + asset_assignments._id
- sub_tenant_id + driver_analytics.avg_safety_score
