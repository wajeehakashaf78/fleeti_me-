Collection: DailyAnalytics
Purpose: Per-day aggregates per asset/driver: distance, duration, idle, over-speeding, fuel, scores.
Use for: Daily totals and day-by-day trends for a vehicle or driver.
Do not confuse with: Not per-trip rows (Trip); not all-time driver KPIs (Driver analytics).
Tenant field: partial (missing on some rows) - scope through a join to Asset or Driver
IDs: asset_id (->Asset._id); driver_id (->Driver._id); date (the day, indexed)
Default sections: DailyAnalytics_base
Notes:
- The day is the field `date` (indexed). `timesptamp` is a misspelled copy - never use `timestamp` (does not exist).
- 61 rows are unrelated call-centre agent stats (agentId, no asset_id) - any asset/driver filter excludes them.
Sections:
- DailyAnalytics_base: daily distance (meters), duration, idle, over_speeding, scores
