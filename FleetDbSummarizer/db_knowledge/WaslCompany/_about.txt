Collection: WaslCompany
Purpose: Saudi WASL company registration for a sub-tenant - commercial record, manager contacts, and the company wasl_key returned by WASL.
Use for: Whether the company is registered on WASL, company identity / CR number, manager contact, Success/Failed reply, Active/Inactive status.
Do not confuse with: Not vehicles or drivers. Not GPS packets. Keys here are reused when registering vehicles/drivers and when pushing locations.
Tenant field: yes (sub_tenant_id)
IDs: sub_tenant_id; wasl_key (->Device.wasl.company_wasl_key)
Default sections: WaslCompany_base
Enums:
- status: Active | Inactive
- reply: Success | Failed
- register_type: Company | Individual
- activity: DEFAULT | SPECIALITY_TRANSPORT | TOW_CAR
- response: Success | identityNumber is not valid | No Response From Wasl (seen)
Notes:
- wasl_key is the company reference used by WASL and often cached on Device.wasl.company_wasl_key.
- reply / response show registration outcome (Success / Failed).
Sections:
- WaslCompany_base
- WaslCompany_extra: other fields (audit: created/updated by/at, and less common fields)
